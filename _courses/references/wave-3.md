取极小的一段空气柱进行分析，则当声波传播时，空气柱压强的变化量：
<span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="\begin{align}&#10;\Delta p &amp;=-B \frac{\Delta V}{V}\\ \\&#10;&amp;=-B \frac{(x_{2}+s_{2}-x_{1}-s_{1})-(x_{2}-x_{1})}{x_{2}-x_{1}} \\&#10;&amp;=-B \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x}&#10;\end{align}" data-display="true"><code>\begin{align}&#10;\Delta p &amp;=-B \frac{\Delta V}{V}\\ \\&#10;&amp;=-B \frac{(x_{2}+s_{2}-x_{1}-s_{1})-(x_{2}-x_{1})}{x_{2}-x_{1}} \\&#10;&amp;=-B \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x}&#10;\end{align}</code></span>
 对空气柱受力分析，它受到的合力：
 <span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="\begin{align}&#10;F_{net}&amp;=(p_{0}+\Delta p_{1})A-(p_{0}+\Delta p_{2})A \\  \\&#10;&amp;=\left( B (\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x})_{2} - (\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x})_{1} \right)A\\&#10;&amp;=BA \Delta \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x}&#10;\end{align}" data-display="true"><code>\begin{align}&#10;F_{net}&amp;=(p_{0}+\Delta p_{1})A-(p_{0}+\Delta p_{2})A \\  \\&#10;&amp;=\left( B (\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x})_{2} - (\frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x})_{1} \right)A\\&#10;&amp;=BA \Delta \frac{\Delta s}{\Delta x}&#10;\end{align}</code></span>
 它的质量：
 <span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="m=\rho \Delta xA" data-display="true"><code>m=\rho \Delta xA</code></span>
 牛顿第二定律：
<span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="a= \frac{B}{\rho} \frac{\Delta (\Delta s/\Delta x)}{\Delta x}" data-display="true"><code>a= \frac{B}{\rho} \frac{\Delta (\Delta s/\Delta x)}{\Delta x}</code></span>
<span class="course-math" data-tex="x\to {0}" data-display="false"><code>x\to {0}</code></span>：

<span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="\frac{\partial^2s}{\partial t^2}=\frac{B}{\rho} \frac{\partial^2s}{\partial x^2}" data-display="true"><code>\frac{\partial^2s}{\partial t^2}=\frac{B}{\rho} \frac{\partial^2s}{\partial x^2}</code></span>
<span class="course-math course-math-display" data-tex="\implies v= \frac{\partial x}{\partial t}=\sqrt{ \frac{B}{\rho} }" data-display="true"><code>\implies v= \frac{\partial x}{\partial t}=\sqrt{ \frac{B}{\rho} }</code></span>
